Stochastic Modelling of Material Tensor Fields

05

Developed probabilistic models of how material properties vary in strength and direction, while preserving their physical structure. Two publications cover symmetric positive-definite tensors and elasticity tensor fields.

My contribution

  • Built random tensor models that preserve positive definiteness and prescribed spatial symmetries, both for individual material samples and their mean.
  • Used Lie algebra representations to control strength and orientation independently, with additional control of eigenstrain for elasticity tensors.
  • Modelled uncertain thermal conductivity in a human proximal femur, demonstrating how strength, orientation and symmetry affect heat conduction.
  • Introduced an elasticity metric for comparing and interpolating fourth-order tensor fields, illustrated with a spatial field of orthotropic Kelvin matrices.

How it works

  1. 1Describe variation in material strength and orientation
  2. 2Build random tensor models that preserve positivity and chosen symmetries
  3. 3Represent conductivity and elasticity as spatially varying material properties
