What I work on

I develop computing models and algorithms, based on data and physics. I have led diverse projects, from materials to agricultural sciences, that not only address deep research questions in predictive sciences but also emphasise on bridging the gap between theory and practice.

My areas of expertise include surrogate modelling, probabilistic machine learning, uncertainty-aware predictions, Monte Carlo methods, Bayesian statistics, stochastic modelling, data assimilation, and edge AI.
