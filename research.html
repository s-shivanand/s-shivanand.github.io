Research

I develop probabilistic and statistical methods for computational models, with an objective of improving efficiency, accuracy and reliability of predictions under uncertainty.

How sure are we?

Modelling

Quantify how noisy measurements and incomplete knowledge affect a model’s predictions.

Can we compute smarter?

Forward pass

Decrease number of expensive forward passes, yet obtain accurate statistical and probabilistic answers.

What can data reveal?

Inverse pass

Work backwards from observations to learn hidden properties of a system, using Bayesian statistical inference.
