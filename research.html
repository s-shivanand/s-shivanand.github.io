The thinking underneath

Better predictions
under uncertainty.

My research asks how we can learn from physics & data, use simulations wisely, and understand how much confidence to place in a prediction.

How sure are we?

Quantify how imperfect measurements and assumptions affect a model’s predictions. The technical name is uncertainty quantification.

Can we compute smarter?

Combine quick approximations with detailed simulations to get useful statistical answers at a lower computational cost.

What can data reveal?

Work backwards from observations to learn hidden properties of a system, using Bayesian statistical inference.
